TODOS, GENERADOS_PRIORITARIOS and REVISION_UE_PRIORITARIOS are combined. The most recent review prevails for each country/type/side. The original catalogs keep the historical variants.

Each country shows ID front, ID back and the passport data page, even when an image is missing. Passport cards are shown separately.
Statuses: image available; document identified but image missing; national ID not issued/cancelled with source; other format; existence to be confirmed. Not obtaining an image is never automatically turned into non-existence. A PRADO record may correspond to a historical model.
Photo and MRZ describe the image/model shown. A secondary portrait includes a recognizable ghost or lenticular image. An empty portrait area is distinguished from a photograph. Demos have featureless portraits; their MRZ have not been validated.
No image: the characteristics of the model are shown when there is documentary evidence, with source and location if known. A characteristic confirmed on the document does not automatically confirm its presence on both sides. Passports without an image show the forecast of the ICAO MRP standard, marked PER STANDARD: it does not verify the country's model. The remaining data stay to be confirmed. MRZ is not deduced from the presence of a chip, QR or barcode. Brazil keeps the physical framing of the MRZ pending verification against an issued card. The new demos include an Original link to compare the reference.
The list does not attest coverage of all issues or the validity of the documents. Images keep the sources and conditions of the original deliveries.

GLOBAL EXAMPLES
ID front with photo and MRZ absent or to be confirmed; if the MRZ is to be confirmed, that status is kept. ID back with MRZ, with or without a face photo: requires an MRZ documented on that side, with no pending positions. For the back, the presence of a photo does not condition the assignment of the global example. Passport with photo and MRZ: assigned according to the catalog model or, if the image is missing, according to the ICAO MRP standard, expressly stating that the country's model remains unverified. Explicit evidence of the absence of an MRZ prevents the assignment. They do not replace the country reference nor count as new national images. No passport without an MRZ has been identified in the references gathered; not all issues, historical models or emergency documents are certified. See pasaportes-mrz.json for the breakdown.
