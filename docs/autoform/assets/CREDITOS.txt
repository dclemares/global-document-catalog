Visual assets of the autofill prototype

DOCUMENTS (assets/catalog/*.webp)
AI recreations of identity documents and passports, generated from public references
(PRADO sheets from the Council of the EU and other sources linked in the global document catalog).
All data is fictitious, the portraits have no features, and the MRZ lines are illustrative and not
validated for OCR. They cannot be used to verify identities or documents. Sources and limits:
  ../LEEME.txt  and  ../index.html (catalog)
The white background of each image has been cropped out and the images saved as WebP with transparency.

ZONES OF EACH DOCUMENT (layouts.js)
Approximate positions (in %) of photo, text, QR/barcode, chip and MRZ, annotated visually
on each recreation to draw the reference outline for the camera. They are estimates, not measurements.

PASSPORT COVER (assets/passport/cover.webp)
Generic passport cover image supplied for the prototype, cropped with no background.

HAND (animation, assets/hand.webp)
Illustration of a hand supplied for the prototype, with a transparent background.
