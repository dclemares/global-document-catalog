How to read it: “Exists / image missing” is based on a reference for the document. “No national ID issued” is stated with a source. “To be confirmed” means there is not enough evidence. Photo and MRZ describe the side and model shown, not every document of the country. Demos have featureless portraits and illustrative MRZ. No image: “Per source” describes the stated model; “Per standard” is a forecast for MRP passports, with the country’s model still unverified. “To be confirmed” does not mean “No”. A chip, QR or barcode does not confirm an MRZ.
